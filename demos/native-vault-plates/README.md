# E10c · Room 1 crate-push rehearsal

The original Training Vault room calls for two held pressure plates. This bench
starts with one crate on A and the spare at its authored x=1.25 m, z=2.32 m
(room-local Three.js). It exercises the stock Engine 0.1.44 player motor and
Rapier rigid bodies. The original A/B placements and raised triangle meshes
come from the existing Room 1 receipt. The floor is a flat proxy, the round
door has a blocking test box, and no original art or native shard was changed.

## Reproduce
With Blender 4.3.2, Node 24 and a package directory containing the pinned
engine and its Rapier dependency, from the repository root:

```sh
D=/tmp/vault-plates
blender -b -t 6 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python demos/native-vault-plates/build.py -- "$D"
WSC_NODE_PROJECT=/path/to/pinned/node/project node --experimental-transform-types \
  demos/native-vault-plates/sample.mjs "$D/geometry.json" "$D/proof.json" "$D/replay.json"
blender -b -t 6 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python demos/native-vault-plates/render.py -- "$D"
```

The room generator populates the Blender renders with recorded crate
transforms. The bulkhead mesh is static even where the test box is open.
The compact flush insert GLB is in `public/assets/wildshard-art`. Its frame
and tread are separate, keep the original 2.00/1.77 m outlines, and use the
original pivot at floor level. The two example placements are x=±2.2 m,
z=−2.35 m, y=.112 m. The 0.8 mm relief is visual; the bench uses uninterrupted
floor collision. The inset does not model physical plate depth or travel.

## What worked, and what did not

* The raised tread is 171.25 mm above the normal floor. All nine original
  approaches stopped or slid off outside B; none parked on the tread.
* Three of nine recessed runs succeeded: all three lateral alignments at
  0.42 forward input. The crate settled before opening B, entirely on the
  tread, with at least 109 mm footprint clearance. The stock player then
  walked through the open test box aperture and walked the crate back off B;
  the test blocker returned. Higher 0.67 and 0.80 inputs stopped short in
  all six trials within the fixed test budget. This does not establish a
  comfortable puzzle with free steering.
* Model the crate as a sliding puzzle prop: yaw is free, roll/pitch are
  constrained, and the test mass is about 140 kg. The scripted player
  issues ordinary movement; it does not set crate state after the initial
  spawn. The fit check requires support and containment, not just overlap.
  A is kept parked in this test. Crate-only logic does not prove the
  player-as-weight case. No multiplayer, door animation, or phone play.
* Proposed next check: remove the delicate analog requirement without
  moving authored starts, and run a human-speed controller test. A support
  plane alone did not solve every push. Don't ship the bench ownership
  code as native integration without testing shared occupancy.

The complete repeatable trace summary is checked in as
`project/layouts/e10c-native-vault-plates-proof.json`. Raw sampled poses are
regenerated by the command; no temporary geometry dumps or donor copies are
required.
