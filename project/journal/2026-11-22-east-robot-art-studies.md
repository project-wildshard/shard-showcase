# East robot art studies

The first East bands need distinct silhouettes at player scale. These are static concept-to-model studies for the existing catalog rows WSC-ACTOR-001 Crate Mite and WSC-ACTOR-002 Pallet Jack. They include no rig, clips, encounters, client integration or collision. The 10 m Mite check was a portrait Blender render with no bloom, not a device benchmark.
