# E8b: mark both entrances to Big Pallet's pit

One enclosed amber shoulder lantern repeats four times around the
existing geometry without touching it. The original rim, floor,
opening widths and two interior pillars stay unchanged. Any retouch
to the weathered enamel can be made in the compact Blender recipe.

All ten paths through the pit preserve the same measurements with
or without the new art, and all stay grounded. The four off-lane
paths stop at the new shoulder lanterns. This verifies geometry
and player movement in a flat stock fixture only, not the boss.
