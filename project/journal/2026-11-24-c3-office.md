# Gauntlet C3: Foreman's office and wall sector

E11d and archived Round 5 `layout.js` / `lab.js` define the blockout.
The 48-degree module recreates the two original diagonal ramps and the
+1.5 and +3 m terraces up to the 6 m ring wall. The 7 × 5 m balcony
spans y=6.0 to 6.5 m. Its axis passes through the wall and joins the
10 × 10 m office volume. The single side guard remains on its archived
side. Window finishes, ribs and fascia are inferred dressing.

The true centerline cut shows the inner edge at r=37.51 m, over +3 m.
The fight floor starts at r=28 m, 9.51 m farther inward. The 3.5 m
vertical dimension is a comparison, not a move or collision result.
The view from inside the floor uses a 1.70 m eye above the +0.05 plate.

```sh
blender -b -t 6 --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python scripts/blender/showcase-studies/c3_office_sector.py -- \
  /absolute/scratch/c3-office
```

The GLB (176,668 bytes, 2,976 triangles) uses local +X radial toward
the office, with origin at map floor center (146,186). Its Blender
reimport supplies both stills. A sourced SVG section and JSON receipt
are generated by the same script. No Gold, actor, authored drop,
runtime collision, waves, scoring or complete C3 court are included.
