# Five zones renovated after the approved three

Jake hand-tuned Signal Dunes, Nalati and Sky Reach, then asked for the
other five to follow them. Two Opus agents rewrote Mossgate, Brasscap,
Driftwood, Pine Hollow and Nine Dragon with what he'd changed:

- Shape opens with one paragraph: the cube, and four entrances mid-side
  each with a road off to the rest of the MMO, in the zone's own terms.
- Use the whole cube, and name every way across.
- A short Features section of plain bullets.
- The final boss is not led: an epic final boss, nothing more (Pine
  Hollow keeps "walks only at night", from the original design).
- No plain weapons: each gets a zone-specific twist.
- A few vivid enemy kinds, and vivid mini dungeons, two per zone.

They run about 100 to 106 lines, like Sky Reach. What went is mostly
boss set-ups (Driftwood's sealed stone ring, Brasscap's swallowed
4:15) and plain weapons and enemies.
