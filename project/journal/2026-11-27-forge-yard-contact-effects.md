# Forge Yard localized hit feedback
Replace the generic center flash and large player cut wedge with an original socket-driven ribbon and a short event-driven contact cue. Each weapon's authored `grip_socket` and `tip_socket` feed the ribbon on its swing window. Confirmed damage spawns a few warm, tapering sparks on the attacker-facing side of the rival. Reduced frontal hits use a separate cool guard cue. No full-body white overlay and no cue on a whiff.
The impact location is a bounded presentation anchor based on rival radius, weak-socket height and the attacker's bearing. It does not indicate exact blade intersection. No changes to damage, cover, attacks or variants. Geometry and tints are generated by local source with a bounded mesh/particle pool and no image dependencies.
