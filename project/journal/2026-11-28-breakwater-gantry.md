# Signal Breakwater cargo gantry

Build an original dock gantry from reusable steel members, pier feet and a service cabin, with a suspended cargo cradle. The 18-second loop lifts, moves 5.6 m, pauses to lower and returns without a jump. Each cable extends to its moving anchor. The cabin's cyan glass and the amber carriage markers make the stationary and moving machinery distinct.

The first staging attempt disappeared behind the portrait HUD; lower the truss profile and move the foundation toward the quay while keeping every piece outside the duel boundary. The gantry adds 1,788 triangles, 129 instances and 19 batches, with no new textures, lights or collision bodies. It is mounted only on Signal Breakwater.

Run the Forge Yard unit suite and bundle, then `breakwater-gantry-smoke.mjs` against the local demo. Tests sample the entire cycle and assert the animated assembly stays outside the combat footprint. In 402 × 874 Chromium, the first cargo frame was inside the camera and a trusted touch approach moved Walker 2.07 m; both actor center points remained in frame. The overhead rail can sit behind the HUD. The indoor yard starts without the gantry and is unchanged. No physical hardware FPS measurement or game deployment.
