# Training Vault Room 3: a vertical section

The room order in E10c is puzzle, sealed fight, climb. This is the climb, not the fight. D41 supersedes the draft 10 m header and gives the full Vault x151…221, world y −50…−30, z−99…−29. The study fits one corner of that box; its coordinates are an illustrative layout, not the native room placement.

The route rises through the reused ladder, three schematic ledges, the original sagging rope walkway, a static split lift and a final round bulkhead. No mesh is stretched. Ray samples at the two centerline end boards establish their exact local height and place both at the proposed lower landing. These samples don't prove usable width or bridge traversal. Room 1's shell materials, metal plates and door class are reused without changing Room 1. The winch casing and rails are section cues only.

The fixed lift frame stays at −44.5 m. The two deck poses are −44.5 and −40.5 m; the lower moving shaft ends at −49.459 m (0.541 m above the allowed box floor). That is an envelope measurement, not working clearance or a functioning winch, guide, or collider. The Gauntlet export is a measured staging donor, not an approved W6a parameter change.

From the repository root:

    blender -b -t 6 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/blender/showcase-studies/vault_climb_room.py -- /tmp/vault-room-03

WSC_METADATA_ONLY=1 verifies donors, exact sampled geometry and world bounds without rendering. The JSON records the two deck states and unchanged GLB hashes. Keep the Python composition and JSON, not a duplicate room mesh archive. There is no gameplay or private yard deployment.
