# C2 The Pit (WSC-SCENE-007): environment and lift fit

This first Gauntlet scene covers only C2. The 82 × 44 m plan has
an 82 × 37 m main floor, +2.5 m north ledge, two 7 m wide descent
wedges from z197 to 205.5, D3 and D4 side recesses and a G2 exit
stub. The original E11 image guides color, light masts and the
orange chevron banner; the Round 5 layout and lab give dimensions.
The separately authored 5 m boundary uses the existing Gauntlet
court bay's ribbed metal language without scaling or copying it.

The fixed frame and moving deck are the two existing
WSC-KIT-009 GLBs, checksum-pinned in the layout. They sit at world
x/z 55/229. Rather than cover the shaft with a floor plane, the
generator divides the slab around a 7.2 m clear hole with a
bottom at y −5.6. The guide reaches −4.959 when flush, and the
deck is at +4 m in the exported art pose. The measurement passes
with 0.86 m to the inner face of the authored south boundary
and 2.82 m from the frame to the nearest crate face at z222.
The banner is cantilevered from the south wall. The checks are
static geometry checks, not gameplay collision.

```sh
blender -b -t 6 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python scripts/blender/showcase-studies/gauntlet_pit.py -- /tmp/wsc-c2
python scripts/art/render-c2-lift-section.py /tmp/wsc-c2
```

The script reimports the GLB before making the Blender renders.
It contains named groups, no textures, and no animations. The
charts are proof views, not a runtime interface. No enemies, host,
running lift, triggers, progression, level lighting, or C1/C3
implementation was added. World origin for this local GLB is
x/y/z 55/0/212; x east, y up, z south in glTF.
