# Pine Hollow and Nine Dragon, second pass

With four descriptions approved (Signal Dunes, Nalati, Sky Reach,
Driftwood), Jake asked for Pine Hollow and Nine Dragon to be renovated
again in that taste. Two Opus agents got his Driftwood and Sky Reach
steers in his own words: go big in scope and systems, never in a pile
of specifics; not leading ("for example …, and more"); no boss hints;
vague enemies in MMO level bands with an endgame; cool weapons, never
gimmicks; no combat systems he didn't ask for.

- Pine Hollow's big idea is now two forests: night is a second forest
  as large as the day one. Mononoke's Night-Walker became "the old
  forests", since it hinted at the boss. Levels stay 1–15.
- Nine Dragon is nine cities stacked; "nine street levels, nine
  dragons" went, being a boss hint. Typhoons drop to weather.

Judgment calls left for Jake: Pine Hollow's level range and its third
(night-only) dungeon, and Nine Dragon's firework launcher and the
pagoda under the crown.

Jake then named what gives Pine Hollow its heart: photoreal graphics,
a real forest full of forest things (ponds, beaver dams, rivers,
hills, forest houses, mills, camps), and hunting that is ranged only,
with crossbows, rifles and pistols. So the bow, the arrows and every
melee weapon went, and the agent's "two forests" idea came out.
Ranged only means you always hold a gun, so under the brief's camera
rule this zone plays almost entirely in first person.
