# Native Mite attacks

The first draft inherited unrelated Grey Blob strikes. Give the Mite explicit
native temperament and two concrete abilities with its own tag. The staged
format 2 product passed static validation. In a real pinned headless run,
378 ticks proved both named abilities winding up and landing. The player
walked 10.72 m and health went from 100 to 64. This does not verify the
native client, sword hits, dormancy, or mobile rendering.
