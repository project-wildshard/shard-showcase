# First-person iron work sword

Replaced the flat blade and blocky hand with a forged fuller, bright edge,
brass guard, wrapped grip and articulated-looking fingers on a leather
gauntlet. Geometry and timing are procedural with no image dependencies.
Windup, strike and recovery share the 148 ms damage trigger. The HUD marks
only confirmed hits. Portrait framing keeps the neutral blade in view and
off the aim point.

Eight Node tests pass. In the desktop browser preview I played the actual
fight with the new view, scored the kill, and walked over the Servo. This
does not replace the native engine's stock first-person view.
