# Brasswick concept art, first nine

CR4's candidates are in `support/showcase/candidates/`: three subjects
(the square, the Blast Wall gate, a Crate Fields fight) in three
takes each, made with Codex's image tool from Cratefall's golden
frames.

- Repainting the frames worked better than I expected. Cratefall's
  density and composition survive, and the brass-and-neon look is
  gone. In its place is the Kitchen Sink's orange and grey: sandstone
  walls, striped awnings, hard-hat robots.
- Take 2, which adds a round-11 mockup as the palette reference, lands
  closest to the Kitchen Sink. Take 3 is the most cinematic.
- Only the gate shots invent a town wall Cratefall doesn't have in
  that form, so they're the most "new design" of the nine.
- PNGs are about 3 MB each, so the repo gets 1536-wide JPEGs and the
  full files wait in `tmp/` for Jake's picks.
