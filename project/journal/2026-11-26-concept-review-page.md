# Concept candidates and the review page

All 81 candidates are made: nine each for the eight zones (ZC2) and
nine for Brasswick (CR4). They're on one review page for Jake
(https://claude.ai/artifact/MZy8tCyogYCyDbGXJHc8hB).

- Each zone got a different look without my asking, because the prompt
  only said "read zone.md, its look section is the art direction".
  Mossgate, Driftwood and Sky Reach came out stylised. Signal Dunes
  and Pine Hollow came out painterly to near-photoreal. Nine Dragon
  came out grey rain with little neon. The page flags the last three,
  since Jake's zone bar wants charm.
- The page saves picks in the artifact's own store, one document per
  zone, in pick order, so pick 1 becomes `concept-1.png`. Copying the
  picks into each clean room is a script run once he's done.
- The page also lists the calls the description writers made on their
  own (new raider factions, Pine Hollow's hatchet, Nalati's boss
  swap), so he reviews art and story in one pass.
