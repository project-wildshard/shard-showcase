# W4b: make the real stand earn its place

Use the previously exported release stand and gate, not another variant.
A stock player can walk up on the flat shoulder and release all four balls
with one valid interaction on the native lever's authored latch. Visiting,
pressing too far away or pressing through an opaque barrier leaves the gate
shut. The resulting nine fallen pins were measured from the physics poses;
no saved score, automatic reset or new game integration is claimed.
