# Signal Dunes, opened up

Jake rewrote the brief for Signal Dunes' description: an open desert
planet (Dune, Dune: Part Two, Dune: Awakening, Breath of the Wild's
deserts), with no quest steps, no named boss, no east and west split
and no dusk story.

- The description went from 121 lines to 58. What's left is his list
  (caravans, the whip-maker, camels, a bounty board, secrets, levels
  1 to 15, a mini dungeon, a multi-step main quest), the 500 m cube
  rules, "filled with biomes" and "five or more epic set pieces".
- The HUD section is gone too: the shared brief covers the HUD now,
  and the old "built for portrait" line contradicted its landscape
  layout.
- It's the same move as the first Cratefall prompt ("vague beats
  detailed"), now for a zone. The other seven descriptions are still
  the detailed drafts.
