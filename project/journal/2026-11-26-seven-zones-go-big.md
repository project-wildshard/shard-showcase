# Seven zones, go big or go home

Jake's new Signal Dunes description became the pattern for the other
seven: short, open-ended, real films and games named as inspirations,
five or more set pieces that pass the ten-second-clip test, and one
huge idea only that zone could have. Two Opus agents rewrote them from
each zone's old draft and SOURCES, keeping its vibe.

- Every one keeps its DNA: Brasscap's looping train, Sky Reach's
  falling, Driftwood's ringed planet and toon look, Mossgate's cosy
  storybook, Pine Hollow's night folklore, Nalati's horses, Nine
  Dragon's ink-and-neon stack.
- Their big new ideas are things like a freefall down Nine Dragon's
  well, a wild herd of hundreds of horses, driving Brasscap's train
  yourself, a sky creature with an island on its back, and a tide
  pulled by the planet.
- What went: every NPC name, quest step, named elite and boss name.
  That's on purpose, the same cut Jake made to the Dunes. The old
  detailed drafts are in git history (`3a16b4f`) if he misses one.
- They run 80 to 84 lines against the Dunes' 75.
