# Wildshard art checkpoint

The Town Gate plaza shell and six reusable props are ready for art review.
This checkpoint contains only self-contained glTF 2.0 binaries and the
review receipt. It does not integrate assets into the game, change
gameplay, or claim PS5 quality. Source projects and review renders stay
out of the repository to keep the checkout lean.

Added WSC-MODULE-029, a reusable shuttered window for a recessed wall
opening. The export is 370,596 bytes, 3,672 triangles and nine
primitives. Town Gate preview instances are for art review only; no
game placements or gameplay changed.

Added the Town Gate merchant house as a standalone WIP. The arched
entrance and cross-gabled terracotta roof replace the graybox in the
art preview only. Matched plaza review improved from 2.65 to 3.75/5.
The export is 1,627,752 bytes, 21,124 triangles and 14 primitives.
No game integration or new gameplay placements.

Added the 22 m obelisk, clock and basin as three separate preview
meshes (3,052,256 raw bytes together). W01 supports the visual pairing
but the clock and basin are not W0b gameplay requirements. The basin
has still water; there are no active jets, colliders or placements.

Added the art-only Tinker Rook frontage WIP: 663,252 raw bytes and
7,460 triangles. The shop front is a shallow shell; the awning in
the preview is separate. No shop interior, placement or behavior.

Added one East Gate flank tower as art WIP. The 1,976,996-byte
export contains 15,398 triangles and two embedded textures.
The two-tower mockup is review only, not gate fit or traversal.

Added the 2,149,740-byte East Gate passage art WIP. Its Blender
context reuses both flanks from one mesh. Geometry clearance rays
are not a traversal test. No roof vista placement, terrain or
seam gate change.

### Hover Park kicker — visual candidate
One authored small kicker for WSC-MODULE-007: 2.400 m high to match the current D7 plan; the footprint, braced frame and ride-surface curve are visual design proposals. Historical W07 is a material and palette cue, not the geometry spec. The asset is not a halfpipe or launch wall, and contains no game behavior, collision or placement.
The reproducibly rebuilt standalone GLB is 686,048 raw bytes, 10,732 triangles, 8 material batches and one embedded image. This is export size, not measured transfer. Keep render staging and authoring source outside runtime Git.

### Ten-model art batch
Four East yard props (robot dock, caged gooseneck, low barrier, tall industrial lamp), four West town props (spool, barrel, wrench sign, lookout bench), and two taller Hover Park kicker classes. These are standalone visual candidates with normalized GLBs, visual inspection of Blender reimports, and byte-identical rebuilds. No gameplay placement, collision or rideability claims. The 3.6 m and 4.8 m kicker variants preserve the preview run-up when toe-aligned; the new chassis each have a distinct silhouette. Keep authoring files and review renders out of the runtime checkout.

### Seam, practice range, and course art
Ten standalone visual models in 3,146,816 raw GLB bytes. Seam: reusable X-braced leaf, striped steel post, straight striped wall bay; comparative fit is illustrative. Range: separate plank, unlit bound straw bale and padded soft dummy. Course: notched flag, post-mounted cyan hoop, station marker #2 and one timber rail bay. Blender reimports and visual proofs checked; no geometry placement, collisions, motion or gameplay. Known raw export bytes are not the deployed zone download.
