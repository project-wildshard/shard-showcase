# Wildshard art checkpoint

The Town Gate plaza shell and six reusable props are ready for art review.
This checkpoint contains only self-contained glTF 2.0 binaries and the
review receipt. It does not integrate assets into the game, change
gameplay, or claim PS5 quality. Source projects and review renders stay
out of the repository to keep the checkout lean.

Added WSC-MODULE-029, a reusable shuttered window for a recessed wall
opening. The export is 370,596 bytes, 3,672 triangles and nine
primitives. Town Gate preview instances are for art review only; no
game placements or gameplay changed.
