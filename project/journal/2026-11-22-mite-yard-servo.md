# Standalone Mite yard: salvage completes the loop

One Servo appears at the point of defeat after the breakup starts to settle.
It has a procedural dark steel body, copper windings and an emissive cyan
cap. Walk within 1.1 m of the drop to pick it up (E1e baseline). It can only
be taken once and expires 60 seconds after landing. Reset clears the counter.

Six deterministic tests cover the encounter and one-time, delayed, expiring
salvage. This proves the standalone pickup, not an addition to the native
Wildshard pack or a second native enemy.
