# E8d · One view of the twelve-frame trophy wall

Reuses the exact twelve shared mount instances and wall backing at
their archived South Plaza transforms. Fits nine existing candidate
trophies together: Big Pallet, the three Night Shift designs, Warden,
Titan, two proposed cache marks, and the Foreman's Whistle as an
illustrative choice. Two unspecified elite positions and the spare are
intentionally empty. No claim that any display position or drop is final.

Nine rays per mount (108 total) hit the fir panel. The frame backs
overlap the panel by 2–4 mm at the sampled points; the reward meshes
have 8 mm forward clearance and at least 55 mm to the inset edges.
All nine rewards returned nine visible surface samples from the
shared 1.74 m-high review camera. Big Pallet is displayed at 0.50×
and the whistle at 2.75×, without changes to either source mesh.
The three Night Shift outlines reuse their authored cuts; the other
six use simplified locked envelopes for review only.

The receipt records source hashes, display-space transforms in both
Blender and glTF axes, and reserved positions. Apply a display
transform to local mesh coordinates, replacing (not adding to) the
existing gallery offset on Cache II. No duplicated GLBs, Blender
scene files or renderer caches, and no installed map, game ownership,
perk, reward ID, label implementation or phone validation.

```sh
blender -b -t 6 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python scripts/blender/showcase-studies/trophy_wall_fit.py -- /tmp/e8d-wall
cmp /tmp/e8d-wall/receipt.json project/layouts/e8d-wall-fit.json
```

Use WILDSHARD_VALIDATE_ONLY=1 to skip rendering; the receipt is identical.
