# Reframe the live counter before the hit

A camera-only probe runs the pinned reference build and its native charge, forward dodge and sword counter. Unlike the previous frozen offsets, a bounded solver runs during the action using the current Rook and Jack sockets. It gradually turns and widens before damage, then hands back to stock in recovery. The prototype never changes the physical camera position or the camera rig's movement yaw, damage, time scale or either actor's action. A frozen projection of the original camera on each sampled pose is the control.

At the damaging tick, 577 Jack vertices projected outside the stock viewport; none projected outside with the prototype. Stock clipped one of the fighters in five of eight fixed samples. The prototype clipped neither in those eight samples. In this run it began correcting roughly 133 ms before damage and reached the 12° turn and 8° vertical widening by contact. Both rigs remained inside at contact, but Rook was close to the right controls. These counts do not establish full on-screen silhouettes between samples, HUD legibility or visual occlusion.

An actual call to the game's look-drag input method and an actual sprint-button hold were separate scripted controls. Both suppress the applied assist immediately. That proves that the assist stops writing offsets; it does **not** prove a comfortable transfer. In particular, the first scripted drag advanced stock yaw by 1.37° while the gate removed a 10.5° display offset and 5.33° of widening in that same frame. That is enough to visibly counterturn on a small drag. The sprint input is intent, not an assertion that the combat-locked character sprinted. A drag-aware transfer, blocker cases and a physical phone pass are still required. The stock gold Broken ring is intentionally unchanged.

Reproduce against the pinned loopback build with `native-probe.mjs FABLE_CHECKOUT DEPENDENCY_PROJECT live-framing none`, then repeat with `look` and `sprint`. Run `live-framing-proof.py NONE_DIR LOOK_DIR SPRINT_DIR` against the three printed output paths, and `node counter-lens.test.mjs` for mirrored direction, finite rates, near-plane rejection and the manual gate's release buffer. This is a scripted 402 × 874 Chromium test in a disposable save. No Fable images, build, assets, or source changes were committed.
