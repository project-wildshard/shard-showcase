# W11a: one switch for the West lights at dusk

Eight previously fitted positions share four point lights in the
stock light-bank fixture. Three are the Stable/West gate lanterns,
four surround the obelisk, and one reads the Movers record board.
The four lights are allocated once and move to the nearest active
anchor in each bank instead of adding a light per fixture.

Two passes reproduced the receipt. The 24-minute stock clock switched
at 20:00 elapsed and cleared the four lights at 24:00 elapsed. All
eight anchors were selected from a nearby view during a restored night
sample. This checks clock, schema and light allocation only; it is
not the installed client plan, native rendering or a phone budget test.
