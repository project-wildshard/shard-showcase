# W5a: the halfpipe as a two-wall launch channel

The WSC-PROP-030 channel keeps the original D7 wall positions and
8 m center, with small amber and teal cues 2 m before each toe.
The inlay is a separate, collision-free model; the one archived wall
GLB is reused twice.

In the stock Engine 0.1.44 bench, 30 scripted round trips up either
inside face and back to center completed at full health. All six runs
that waited to release at the toe carried over the wall instead.
A slower return and a second release stopped the board, rather
than ending the replay while it was still moving. No trick or
scoring, native placement, or phone feel claim.
