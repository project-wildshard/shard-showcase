# The Showcase MMO Zone

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. It should feel like logging
into an MMO when nobody else is online: a town full of people to talk
to, wild areas full of enemies that get tougher as you go, a mini
dungeon, and a raid-style final boss. It is also a showcase: every fun
thing a game like this can do, packed into one place.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height (250 m below, 250 m above). Fit more
  in by stacking, building interiors and going underground or into the
  sky, never by making it bigger.
- The mini dungeon and the boss arena are interiors reached by portals
  or teleports, but each one physically sits somewhere inside the
  cube, and no two spaces overlap. No portal leads outside the cube.
  Pack the content in cleverly.
- The **west half is peaceful**: no enemies. The **east half is
  combat**. The town's east gate is the way across.
- Day and night, weather and storms. Some enemies only come out at
  night.

## The west

- **The town**: walled, with a gate plaza and a main street. Its
  people include three quest givers (one per level tier), a tinker who
  sells upgrades, a weapons-shop keeper and a stable hand. It also has
  a kill-contract board and a trophy wall.
- A **weapon range** with practice dummies and long ranged lanes.
- A **physics yard**: pressure plates that open a gate, crates,
  see-saws, a ball run.
- A **riding course** with a loaner horse from the stable: rings,
  jumps and a lap timer.
- A **movers run**: moving platforms, lifts, conveyors.
- A **grapple course**, and a long zipline into town.
- A **climb yard**: a scramble wall, ladders and swaying rope bridges.
- **The Rock**: a crag with a squeeze cave and a lookout bench.
- A **hoverboard park**: kickers, a halfpipe, rails, a bowl, tricks.
- Each course keeps your personal best. Secrets, vistas, hidden caches
  and collectibles all over.

## The east

- **Levels 1 to 20**, levelled by fighting. Six level bands of three
  levels each, getting harder the deeper you go, with an elite at each
  band gate. Reaching 20 is a long grind, hours of play.
- **The Training Vault**: the mini dungeon, behind a portal. Four
  rooms: a puzzle, a fight, a climb and its boss, the Vault Warden.
- **The gauntlet**: a wave-survival arena of three sealed courts and
  five waves, with an elite in the middle waves.
- **The Crate Titan's arena**: a raid-style final boss fight in a vast
  hidden arena behind a boss portal. Its road opens after your first
  gauntlet clear.
- **Storm hour**: a quest turns the whole zone to an evening storm
  until you light four beacons.

## Look

Simple, stylised, polished and playful: a warm orange and cool grey
workshop / test-yard palette with cyan accents. It should look
finished, never like placeholder or a grey blockout.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Melee: sword, spear.
- Ranged: bow, crossbow (with a few ammo types), rifle.

## Enemies

The enemies are robots. The zone should have at least these:

- One common kind per band: Crate Mite (L1–3), Pallet Jack (L4–6),
  Plumb Drone (L7–9), Welder Unit (L10–12), Riveter (L13–15), Arc
  Sentry (L16–18).
- Rare variants that turn up among the commons.
- Night-shift variants of each, a little tougher.
- Elites: Big Pallet (L6), the Vault Warden (L12), Foreman Unit 7
  (L18).
- Night elites: Sir Nibbles, Hot Mess, Riveting Rita.
- The final boss: the **Crate Titan** (L20), a 15 m giant made of
  crates. It fights in three phases and has an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- The zone is full of quests. Quest givers show a grey "!" while
  you're too low for a quest and a yellow "!" once you can take it.
- The kill-contract board pays for hunting each enemy kind, elite and
  the boss, with a trophy and a joke title the first time.
- The currency is Scrip. Kills also drop salvage parts. The weapons
  shop sells weapons unlocked by level; the tinker sells upgrades,
  including forge tiers that improve every weapon. Loot, chests and a
  pack to carry it in.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
