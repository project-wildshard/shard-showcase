# The Kitchen Sink

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. It should feel like logging
into an MMO when nobody else is online: a town full of people to talk
to, wild areas full of enemies that get tougher as you go, a dungeon,
and a huge final boss. It is also a showcase: every fun thing a game
like this can do, packed into one place.

## Shape

- One 500 × 500 m zone. Fit more in by stacking, building interiors
  and going underground, rather than by making it bigger.
- The **west half is peaceful**: a walled town, plus playgrounds for
  movement and toys (weapon practice, riding, grappling and ziplines,
  climbing, moving platforms, physics props, a hoverboard park).
  Secrets, vistas and collectibles all over it.
- The **east half is combat**: areas for each level band, getting
  harder as you go deeper, with elites along the way, a small dungeon
  behind a portal, a wave-survival arena, and a final boss arena behind
  a portal.
- Levels 1 to 20. You level up by fighting. The final boss is level 20.
- Day and night, weather and storms. Some enemies only come out at
  night.

## Look

Simple, stylised, polished and playful: a warm orange and cool grey
workshop / test-yard palette with cyan accents. It should look
finished, never like placeholder or a grey blockout.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Melee: sword, spear.
- Ranged: bow, crossbow (with a few ammo types), rifle.

## Enemies

The enemies are robots. The zone should have at least these kinds:

- Common enemies in every level band, a different kind per band.
- Rare variants that turn up among the commons.
- Night-time variants.
- Elites: named, much tougher, a few of them.
- A dungeon boss.
- The final boss: the **Crate Titan**, a giant robot made of crates,
  with an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

The zone should be full of quests. There is a currency, shops,
upgrades, loot and rewards worth chasing.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
