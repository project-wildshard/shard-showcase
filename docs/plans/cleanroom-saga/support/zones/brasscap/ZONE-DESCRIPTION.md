# Brasscap Mesa

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. A railroad town on a mesa
above a deep red canyon at sunset. The Dust Wyrm swallowed the 4:15
to Brasscap, carriages and all; tin bandits hold three camps down the
canyon trail and have taken the line's last train, an armoured
freight that now runs round the canyon as their rolling fort. Sheriff
Brass puts a lever-action rifle in your hands. The centrepiece is the
train: the biggest fights in the zone happen on it, at full speed.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height: the mesa tops sit at road level and
  the canyon cuts deep below them. Fit more in by going down into the
  canyon and into the rock, never by making it bigger.
- **The railroad** sweeps in a long curve along the canyon floor
  between two tunnels in the canyon walls. A train that runs into one
  tunnel comes straight out of the other, so it goes round and round
  without stopping, and anyone riding it comes out with it.
- The mini dungeon is the old mine inside the mesa. It and the
  tunnels physically sit inside the cube; no two spaces overlap.
- **The mesa top is peaceful**: no enemies. **The canyon is combat**,
  tougher the deeper down the trail you go.
- A long sunset, then night, with lanterns in town and the train's
  headlamp on the walls. Dust storms; some bandits ride only at night.

## The mesa

- **Brasscap**, the depot town: the depot and its porch, a water
  tower, a saloon, a general store, a stable, and the wanted poster
  for the Dust Wyrm. Its people include **Sheriff Brass**, who keeps
  the peace, or did, and starts the main quest; the stationmaster,
  who lost the 4:15; a gunsmith who sells guns and upgrades; and a
  stable hand who lends you a horse.
- **The target range** behind the depot: tin targets, bottles on a
  fence, long lanes for the scoped rifle, and shooting from horseback.
- **The rim ride**, a horse trail along the rim with jumps and a lap
  timer, and lookouts over the whole canyon and the line below.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over: mail sacks from
  the 4:15, gold in the creek, wanted posters for every outlaw.

## The canyon

- **Levels 1 to 12**, levelled by fighting. Four bands of three
  levels, tougher the deeper you go, with an elite at each band gate.
- **Three bandit camps** down the trail, the last by the canyon gate
  with a strongbox behind it, and past the gate the drake flats.
- **The Brasscap Mine**, the mini dungeon in the mesa's rock: timber
  galleries, a powder store, a runaway ore-cart ride, and Two-Draw
  Della at the bottom.
- **The line**: the trestle, the cuts, the two tunnels, and the
  armoured train itself (flatcars, boxcars, a gun car, a caboose and
  the locomotive) to fight along, climb on and leap between.
- **The sand pit**, beside the line, where the Wyrm comes up.

## Main quest: The 4:15 to Brasscap

At least these steps:

1. Talk to Sheriff Brass on the depot porch.
2. Try the lever-action on the target posts.
3. Clear the three bandit camps, then open the canyon gate.
4. Cross the drake flats and clear the mine of the bandits' powder.
5. Hear the stationmaster's plan; board the train out of a tunnel.
6. Fight car to car to the locomotive and beat the Tin Baron.
7. The Dust Wyrm hears the train and comes up beside it. Beat it: the
   fight starts on the moving train and ends in the sand pit.
8. Bring the train home to Brasscap and tell Sheriff Brass.

## Look

The art direction is a low-poly sunset canyon: layered red and orange
rock, flat-topped mesas, long purple shadows, a hot gold sky, dust in
the light, weathered wood and brass; faceted, warm and cinematic.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Ranged: the **lever-action rifle**, the zone's signature, a
  six-shooter, a double-barrel shotgun, a scoped long rifle, a bow.
- Melee: a Bowie knife, a miner's pick.

## Enemies

At least these:

- One common kind per band: dust wyrmlings (L1–3) that burrow and
  ram; tin bandits (L4–6), outlaws in hammered tin who draw before
  they fire; sand drakes (L7–9) on the flats; bandit rushers,
  brawlers and marksmen (L10–12) who circle, rush, and shoot from
  ledges and train roofs.
- Rare variants among the commons, and tougher night riders.
- Elites: Big Iron, a brawler who holds the camp by the gate (L6),
  Two-Draw Della, a marksman who holds the mine (L9), and the Tin
  Baron, boss of the bandits, on his train (L12).
- The final boss: **the Dust Wyrm** (L12), a giant sand wyrm that
  swallows trains. A raid-style fight in three phases with an intro
  cutscene; its gullet glows gold after each strike: shoot it then.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests, at least: the mail sacks, the strongbox, a bounty on
  each camp, the stationmaster's lost cargo, the rim ride, the gold.
- Happenings, each signalled first: bandits hitting a supply wagon, a
  dust storm, the train running the gauntlet past a camp.
- The sheriff pays a bounty on each outlaw kind, elite and the Wyrm.
  Coins, rounds and salvage from kills; ammo boxes at every camp. The
  gunsmith sells guns and upgrades by level. Loot, chests and a pack.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
