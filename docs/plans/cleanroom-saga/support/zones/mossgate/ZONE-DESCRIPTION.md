# Mossgate Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a green, mossy hollow in
the hills at first light, and the wild country all around it. Think
Kena: Bridge of Spirits, Ni no Kuni and My Neighbor Totoro. Mist in
the low ground, flowers in the long grass, standing stones on the
downs, and cold braziers waiting to be lit. Every flame you light
brings the morning on, and the land wakes up around you. It should
feel like a storybook come to life, from the first step to the last.

## Shape

It is one zone of a bigger MMO world, joined to its neighbours by
roads, so it fits inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube and never grows.
Four old stone gates sit at the middle of the cube's four sides,
halfway up, each with a road running off the edge to the rest of the
world; nothing beyond needs to exist yet. Both mini dungeons sit
inside the cube too.

- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** Hills and hollows fill
  the full 500 × 500 m footprint and its full height: barrows, the
  quarry pit and drowned places under the bog go down to the cube's
  floor, and the ridge, the crags and the old beacon towers climb to
  its top. Every hill hides a hollow; flat, empty ground is never
  most of the view.
- **Every way across:** thistledown on the wind, a boar at full
  charge, stepping stones over the bog, rope lifts down the quarry,
  felled trees for bridges and tunnels under the barrows.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Filled with wild places** around a peaceful hollow: meadow,
  thicket, bog, quarry, ridge and barrow downs, each with its own
  look, life and danger.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- The Hollow: a walled yard, a pond, a lamplighter, a pedlar who sells
  weapons, and no enemies.
- **Light wakes the land.** Every brazier, lantern and beacon you
  light stays lit, and the mist rolls back from it, flowers open and
  the morning spreads out from it across the land.
- Thistledown as big as a parasol grows on the hilltops: grab a seed
  head, jump, and drift off on the wind across the whole hollow.
- Win a boar over and it's yours to ride: a headlong, snorting charge
  through hedges and over the old stone walls.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over.
- Levels 1 to 10, levelled by fighting.
- Two mini dungeons: one in a great barrow on the downs, lit brazier
  by brazier as you go deeper, and one in a sunken mill under the
  bog, its wheel still turning in the dark water.

## Features

- Braziers that bring the morning.
- Thistledown gliding.
- Boar riding.
- Fire on any weapon.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps that takes you across the whole zone
and ends in an epic final boss fight. The story, the steps and the
boss are yours to design.

## Look

- Stylised low-poly toon, friendly and storybook: cosy, never grim.
- Soft greens and mossy stone, dew and mist in the hollows, warm first
  light on the hilltops, and fire that really burns.
- Days turn, soft rain comes and goes, and wisps glow at night.
- Ni no Kuni as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Melee: a moss blade whose cut roots a foe to the spot; a
  lamplighter's staff, hooked at the top, that carries a flame and
  pulls foes off their feet; a woodsman's axe that fells a tree into a
  bridge; and more.
- Ranged: a thistle bow whose arrows ride the wind on seed fluff, so
  a shot curves round a tree; an ember sling that lobs live coals and
  lights any brazier or bramble they land in; and more.
- Any weapon can take a flame from a brazier: it burns back brambles,
  lights dark places and sends wisps and mosslings scattering.

## Enemies

The enemies should include mosslings that ambush in packs, bog wisps
that lure you off the path, and hooded snuffers, lantern thieves who
come for the flame you carry, and more.

- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 10.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings.
- A bounty board by the Hollow's gate.
