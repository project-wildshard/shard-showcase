# Nine Dragon Stack (九龍疊城)

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: one impossibly dense
megablock filling the zone from black water to an antenna crown in the
clouds. Built like the Kowloon Walled City, climbing like Chongqing,
where a monorail runs through a tower. Chinese, cyberpunk, futuristic.
Think Ghost in the Shell, Cyberpunk 2077, Sekiro's grapple and
Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon. It should feel like the biggest city
ever built, from the first step to the last.

## Shape

It is one zone of a bigger MMO world, joined to its neighbours by
roads, so it fits inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube and never grows.
Four streets enter at the middle of the cube's four sides, halfway up,
each crossing a bridge off the edge to the rest of the world; nothing
beyond needs to exist yet. Both mini dungeons sit inside the cube too.

- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** The city fills the full
  500 × 500 m footprint and its full height, from the black water on
  the cube's floor to the antenna crown at its top: towers on towers,
  streets on roofs. The only gaps are the wells; open sky is never
  most of the view.
- **Nine cities, stacked:** nine street levels from sump to crown,
  each a whole district as dense, varied and worth exploring as a
  zone of its own, with its own places, people, quests, secrets and
  set pieces. Each one feels like the ground: a real street, a sky
  screen overhead, mist at the edges. Only the wells show the drop.
- **Every way across:** stairs and alleys, bridges over the wells,
  the wrist grapple, lifts, cable cars, the monorail and an umbrella
  glide, with rooftop runs as an extra.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, up or down.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- **The wells:** great shafts drop through every level, crossed by
  bridges, with nine levels of neon mist below you.
- **The wrist grapple:** hook any sign, mast or balcony; swing and zip.
- **The freefall:** dive a well through level after level, pop an
  oil-paper umbrella at the last second and glide out.
- **The monorail** runs through a tower: ride it, or fight on its roof.
- You start on a peaceful square at street level. It gets wilder the
  deeper or higher you go.
- Life on every level, for example teahouses, mahjong parlours, night
  markets, shrines, and more.
- Weather with weight: drizzle, and typhoons that take your glide.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles on every level.
- Levels 1 to 20, levelled by fighting.
- Two mini dungeons: a flooded gambling den in the sump, fought room
  by room as the water rises, and a pagoda of humming servers hung
  under the crown, fought floor by floor from its roof to its root.

## Features

- Vertical exploration, sump to crown.
- A wrist grapple.
- An umbrella glide.
- Freefall down the wells.
- A monorail that you can ride.
- Fast travel up the city: a lift, a line, a creature or something
  else; yours to design.
- A city stacked nine streets high: nine whole districts, one on top
  of another, each one the ground.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps that takes you through all nine
levels and ends in an epic final boss fight. The story, the steps and
the boss are yours to design.

## Look

- A Song-dynasty ruled-line ink painting come alive at blue hour: ink
  on every edge, blue-grey washes, cinnabar and gold leaf, silk mist.
- Neon is the only saturated light; it bleeds into the mist and
  streaks across wet stone. Drizzle, and Chinese signs everywhere.
- Ghost in the Shell's Hong Kong, painted in ink, as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Melee: a neon jian whose strokes hang glowing in the mist; a hooked
  guandao that catches a foe and throws them over the rail; and more.
- Ranged: a repeating crossbow whose charged bolts jump between foes
  on wet stone; a firework launcher whose rockets weave down a crowded
  alley, then burst in gold and cinnabar; and more.
- The grapple fights too: yank a foe off a ledge, or yourself to them.

## Enemies

- Fill the city with enemies as you see fit, from the sump to the
  crown, among them neon-visored triads who own the stairwells and
  cyborg hopping vampires.
- Like an MMO, they come in level bands, each with its own part of
  the city: 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12, 13–15 and 16–18.
- Level 20 is the endgame: bosses and endgame content worth coming
  back for.
- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 20.

## Quests

- Side quests from people on every level.
- Happenings, for example a blackout that kills the neon, and more.
- A bounty wall on the square.
