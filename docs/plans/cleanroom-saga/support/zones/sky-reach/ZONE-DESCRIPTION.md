# Sky Reach

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. Grassy islands drift over a
sea of cloud at golden hour, joined by rope bridges and by glowing
bridges that carry only a hoverboard. The crown bridge fell in the
last storm, and on the storm crown something huge has made its nest.
You carry a war fan whose gust throws creatures off the edge, and
everyone here fears the long fall into the clouds.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge, each a stone causeway to
  a small gate isle. Here the causeways just lead off the edge;
  nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. The cloud sea fills the bottom; the
  islands float at many heights above it, the storm crown near the
  top. Fit more in by stacking islands and hollowing out their keels,
  never by making it bigger. The mini dungeon (inside a keel) and the
  storm crown physically sit inside the cube; no two spaces overlap.
- **Falling is the danger.** Every island has an edge, the cloud sea
  is a long way down, and falling off always costs you something.
- **The islands around road level are peaceful**: no enemies. The
  higher you climb, the wilder and stormier it gets.
- The hour is fixed: always golden hour, the sun low and ahead. The
  weather is wind: gusts you can see, updrafts, and a storm over the
  crown until the Storm Roc falls.

## The peaceful isles

- **Sunrest**, the home isle and village: cottages, a sky dock, goats
  on the green. Its people: **Old Tamsin, the bridge-keeper**, who
  starts the main quest; a fan-maker who sells weapons; a board-wright
  who gives you a hoverboard; a goatherd who has lost her flock.
- **The windmill isle**, its sails stopped; its updraft leads up.
- **The hoverboard**: glowing bridges carry only the board, and on
  foot you fall through. Updrafts and rising islets lift you high.
- **The board-wright's runs**, timed ring courses through updrafts,
  and **the goat round-up**: gust the strays home, gently.
- Lookout spires, a hanging garden under an island's lip, bells to
  ring. Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over, many on
  ledges you reach only by board or a brave jump.

## The high isles

- **Levels 1 to 12**, levelled by fighting. Four bands of three
  levels, rising with height, with an elite at each band gate.
- **The Roost**: a crag of nests where the drift rays breed.
- **Three wind vanes** on three isles, stuck still since the storm.
- **The Winchworks**, the mini dungeon in the high step's keel: gear
  rooms, wind shafts, a jammed winch, a howling knot of wind.
- **The high step**, where kite raiders land to strip the isles.
- **The storm crown**: the last, highest island, a ring of standing
  stones around a dais under a bruised, gold-edged storm.

## Main quest: The crown bridge

At least these steps:

1. Talk to Old Tamsin at Sunrest.
2. Get a hoverboard from the board-wright and cross a glowing bridge.
3. Read the bridge-keeper's notes at the lectern on the keeper's isle.
4. Clear the Roost of drift rays and their mother.
5. Turn the three wind vanes with the fan's gust.
6. Ride the windmill isle's updraft up and clear the Winchworks.
7. Drive the raiders off the high step and raise the crown bridge.
8. Cross to the storm crown and beat the Storm Roc, then tell Tamsin.

## Look

The art direction is low-poly grass islands over a cloud sea at golden
hour: a storybook painting of an evening above the clouds. Spring
green grass with gold tips, warm rock keels with hanging roots, white
stone, cream sail cloth, a peach-and-gold sky, and the cyan glow of
the hover bridges and the wind as the one cool accent. Every frame
looks into a warm sun, and nothing in it goes black.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Melee: the **war fan**, the zone's signature (it slashes, sweeps,
  and its gust blows creatures off any edge they stand near), a glaive.
- Ranged: a bow, a crossbow with bolts that knock foes back.

## Enemies

At least these:

- One common kind per band: wild sky goats (L1–3) that ram whatever
  crowds them; drift rays (L4–6), mantas that circle, hang over you,
  then dive; gale wisps (L7–9), knots of wind that burst against you
  and push you toward the edge; kite raiders (L10–12), gliding
  bandits with hooks and crossbows.
- Rare variants, and tougher storm-touched variants near the crown.
  Anything can be knocked off an edge.
- Elites: Old Ramhorn (L3), the Roost Mother (L6), the Howling Knot
  (L9), the Raider Captain (L12).
- The final boss: **the Storm Roc** (L12), lord of the storm crown. A
  raid-style fight in three phases: it stoops out of the storm, beats
  up walls of gale that push you to the edge, then lands on the dais.
  It has an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests from Sunrest, at least: the goatherd's strays, the
  miller's sails, the board-wright's runs, a raider hunt, the bells.
- Happenings, each signalled from afar first: a gust front, raiders
  landing on an island, a goat stranded on a sinking islet.
- A notice board pays for each enemy kind, elite and the boss. Coins,
  feathers, wisp-silk and ray leather from kills. The fan-maker and
  board-wright sell upgrades by level. Loot, chests and a pack.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
