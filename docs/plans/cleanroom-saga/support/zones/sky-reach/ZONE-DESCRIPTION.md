# Sky Reach

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: grassy islands drifting
over an endless sea of cloud, at a golden hour that never ends. Think
Hayao Miyazaki's Castle in the Sky, the sky islands of The Legend of
Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, and Sky: Children of the Light. Rope
bridges and glowing bridges between the islands, a storm crowning the
top of the sky, and a long, long fall under every edge. It should
feel like flying from the first step to the last.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads are stone
  causeways that just lead off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by stacking islands up
  through the cloud banks and hollowing out their keels, never by
  making it bigger. The mini dungeon sits physically inside the cube
  and overlaps nothing else.
- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** Islands fill the full
  500 × 500 m footprint and climb its full height, from the cloud sea
  on the cube's floor to the storm at its top: big islands, small
  ones, islands under islands. From any island the next is a jump, a
  glide or a short board ride away. The gaps are narrow and deep;
  empty sky is never most of the view.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Filled with islands**, each with its own look, life and danger:
  calm around road level, wilder and stormier the higher you climb.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- A village isle with a sky dock, a fan-maker and a board-wright.
- A hoverboard. Glowing bridges carry only the board; on foot you fall
  straight through them.
- The wind is a road: your board rides the rivers of wind you can see
  between the islands, and updrafts throw you high.
- **Falling is the danger.** The cloud sea is a long way down and it
  always costs you. Every fall is a dive: steer it, and catch yourself
  on the board before the clouds catch you, if you can.
- Something huge swims through the sky with an island on its back.
  Catch it on your board, land on it and ride it across the zone.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over.
- Levels 1 to 12, levelled by fighting.
- A mini dungeon in the keel of an island.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps that climbs the whole sky to the top
of the storm and ends in a final boss fight worth the journey. The
story, the steps and the boss are yours to design.

- The final boss lives in the storm that crowns the sky, in view from
  the first step. It throws you off the top, and the fight goes on in
  free fall down the sky and back up on the updrafts. It still fits
  the cube.

## Look

- A storybook painting of an evening above the clouds: the sun low and
  ahead, never moving, and nothing in the frame ever black.
- Warm everywhere, with wind you can always see and one cool accent:
  the cyan glow of the hover bridges and the wind.
- Castle in the Sky as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Melee: the war fan, a glaive and more.
- Ranged: a bow, a crossbow whose bolts knock foes back, and more.
- The war fan's gust blows anything near an edge clean off it, and it
  moves you too: a burst of speed, or a softer fall.

## Enemies

The enemies should include wild sky goats that ram, drift rays that
circle and dive, and gale wisps that push you toward the edge, and more.

- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites are scattered around.
  Anything can be knocked off an edge, and so can you.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 12.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings, seen coming from far across the sky.
- A bounty board.
