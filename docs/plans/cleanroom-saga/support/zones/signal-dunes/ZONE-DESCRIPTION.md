# Signal Dunes

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. A sea of sand in the long
dusk just after sunset. A caravan's riders went out into the dunes
three days ago and never came back. You come over a crest at last
light, the signal tower far off against the afterglow and a bullwhip
on your belt, and help the caravan light the old signal fire that
will call its riders home. Lighting it wakes the
thing that has been hunting them: the Dune Matriarch.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by going under the sand
  and up the tower ridge, never by making it bigger. The mini dungeon
  lies under the dunes, down the dry well; it physically sits inside
  the cube and overlaps nothing else.
- The **east is the caravan's side and peaceful**: no enemies. The
  **west is the deep dunes**, wilder nearer the tower and the basin.
- **The dusk deepens as the story goes on.** Each main-quest step
  sinks the afterglow and brings out more stars, until the Matriarch
  is fought under a starry night with one band of light left. Some
  creatures come out only at full dark. Sandstorms roll through.

## The east

- **Last Light**, the caravan's stone waystation: camel pens, awnings,
  cook fires, and families waiting for the riders. Its people: **Sefa,
  the caravan scout**, who starts the main quest; the caravan master,
  whose son rode out; a whip-maker who sells weapons; a water-seller;
  a camel keeper who lends you a camel to ride; and a star-reader on
  the roof. A bounty board hangs by the gate.
- Timed **sled runs** down slip faces; **whip swings** across gaps.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over: cargo the storms
  scattered, and the riders' letters, which tell what happened.

## The west

- **Levels 1 to 15**, levelled by fighting. Five bands of three
  levels, tougher the deeper west you go, with an elite at each gate.
- **The half-buried caravan**, its logbook, and skitterer nests.
- **The Old Cistern**, the mini dungeon down the dry well: sandstone
  vaults of a town the sand swallowed. A windlass shaft, dry channels
  full of nests, a dark vault you light brazier by brazier with the
  whip, and the Skitter Queen's chamber, where the oil is sealed.
- **The waymarks**, three cold braziers in dangerous places, and **the
  Jackal camp**, raiders who stripped the caravan and took captives.
- **The signal tower** on the highest crest, and below it **the
  basin**, a vast bowl of sand where the Matriarch rises.

## Main quest: Light the signal fire

At least these steps:

1. Talk to Sefa on the crest; she walks you to Last Light.
2. Find the half-buried caravan and read its logbook.
3. Fetch oil from the dry well: clear the Old Cistern and its Queen.
4. Light the three waymarks: pour the oil, crack the whip at the bowl.
5. Free the riders the Jackals took; they saw a giant in the basin.
6. Take the tower back from the Jackals and their chief.
7. Light the signal fire. The Matriarch rises from the basin.
8. Beat the Matriarch; the riders come home by the fire's light.

## Look

The art direction is dark orange dunes just after sunset under an
indigo sky: one low warm light raking across big smooth sand forms,
burnt-orange crests, cool violet hollows, a tall afterglow band and
the first stars. Weathered wood, rope, canvas, iron and sandstone.
Nothing glows on its own except fire, and the signal fire is the
brightest thing in the zone. No glass, crystals, neon or magic glow.
The art direction is shown in the three images in `concept/`.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Melee: the **bullwhip**, the zone's signature (it cracks, double
  cracks to stagger big beasts, pulls small creatures and levers,
  lights braziers, swings you across gaps), a scimitar, a spear.
- Ranged: a horn bow, a long desert rifle.

## Enemies

At least these:

- One common kind per band: sand skitterers (L1–3), burrowing packs
  that burst out at your heels; dune rays (L4–6), gliders that swoop
  at walkers on the crests; dune striders (L7–9), horned grazers that
  paw the sand, then charge; Dust Jackals (L10–12), raiders with
  knives, whips and rifles; basin rays (L13–15), the Matriarch's
  brood.
- Rare variants, and night variants out only at full dark.
- Elites: the Skitter Queen (L6), Old Hornback (L9), Kesh, chief of
  the Jackals (L12), and a night elite, the Pale Sister, a white ray
  that hunts by starlight.
- The final boss: **the Dune Matriarch** (L15), mother of the dusk
  rays. A raid-style fight in three phases: sweeping dives, a
  sandstorm she hides inside, then grounded, where the whip can reach
  her. It has an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests from Last Light, at least: the caravan master's lost
  son, the whip-maker's hides, the water-seller's nests, the
  star-reader's lookout crests, the children's sled runs.
- Happenings: a sandstorm, a Jackal raid on the water train, a strider
  stampede, each signalled across the zone before it starts.
- The bounty board pays for each enemy kind, elite and the boss.
  Coins, hides and ray leather from kills; the whip-maker sells
  weapons and upgrades by level. Loot, chests and a pack.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
