# Signal Dunes

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: one open stretch of a
desert planet. Think Dune and Dune: Part Two, Dune: Awakening, and the
deserts of The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild. Sand to the
horizon, dunes the size of mountains, rock and ruins breaking through,
and the sense that the desert itself is the most dangerous thing on
the planet. It should feel epic from the first step to the last.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by going under the sand
  and up into the rock, never by making it bigger. The mini dungeon
  sits physically inside the cube and overlaps nothing else.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Filled with biomes**, each with its own look, life and danger.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

- Caravans.
- A whip-maker and a weapon shop.
- Rideable camels.
- Every big dune face is a slide: surf it, faster the steeper it gets,
  and launch off the crest.
- Something huge lives in the sand, and it can be ridden: hook it,
  climb it and steer it across the zone, if you dare.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by fighting.
- A mini dungeon.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps that takes you across the zone and
ends in a final boss fight worth the journey. The story, the steps and
the boss are yours to design.

- The final boss is in view from the first step: something on the
  skyline that every player takes for a mountain, and isn't. The fight
  ranges across the zone, not an arena. It still fits the cube.

## Look

- Epic dunes.
- Oases.
- The sand is never still: it streams off dune crests, slides under
  your feet and fills your tracks in behind you.
- Dune: Part Two as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Melee: whips, a scimitar, a spear and more.
- Ranged: a horn bow, a long desert rifle and more.
- Whips also move you: crack one at a rock lip to pull yourself up, or
  at a rider to pull them off their camel.

## Enemies

- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings.
- A bounty board.
