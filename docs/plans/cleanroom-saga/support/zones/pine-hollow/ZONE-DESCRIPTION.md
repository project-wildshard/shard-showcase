# Pine Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. A deep boreal forest valley
of Scots pine, birch and old-growth fir, from dawn ground fog to
lantern-lit night. It is a hunter's zone with an eerie folk tale at its
heart: the Hollow's three waystone lanterns have gone dark, and
something with antlers of light walks the old-growth at night. Quiet,
beautiful and unsettling, never gory. It ends with the forest cleansed
at dawn.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads are forest
  tracks that just lead off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
  You arrive by the south road.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by climbing the crag
  ridge and going down into the caves, never by making it bigger.
- The bear cave and the cabins are interiors, and each physically sits
  inside the cube. No two spaces overlap, and no portal leads outside.
- The **Hollow and the mill hamlet are peaceful**: only grazing game
  that flees. The wilds get more dangerous toward the den and the
  old-growth.
- Dawn, day, golden hour and a moonlit night bright enough to hunt in.
  Dawn ground fog and rain showers, but no thunderstorms or snow. Rain
  sends the herds to shelter under the big trees. Some creatures only
  come out at night, and at any campfire you can wait for nightfall.

## The valley

- **The Hollow**: the bowl where the south road comes in, a crossroads
  and lantern posts along the paths. **Hale**, the old ranger, lives in
  the Hollow cabin; its back wall is the trophy wall.
- **The still pond and the creek**: reeds, a beaver dam, a footbridge,
  a waterfall off the ridge, and a canoe out to a little islet.
- **The Ridge**: granite crags, a ridge cabin, and a fire-lookout tower
  with a vista bench and a zipline down to the Hollow.
- **The Den**: bear country, with a cave whose back tunnels are the
  mini dungeon.
- **The Old-growth**: giant firs and cedars, ground fog and fallen
  giants to walk along. At its heart is the Antler King's clearing:
  standing stones, and lanterns hung in the canopy.
- **The mill hamlet**: a watermill on the creek. **The miller**, **the
  trader**, and the hunting lodge with its contract board.

## Main quest: The Warden's Hollow

At least these steps, each marked on the map and in the world:

1. Hale tells you the lanterns went dark, and what walks at night.
2. **The pond lantern**: a creek and dam puzzle; drain the pool.
3. **The ridge lantern**: climb the lookout, then zipline back down.
4. **The den lantern**: deep in the bear cave, past Old Blackpaw.
5. Follow the Ghost Stag through the night fog to the old-growth.
6. Beat **the Antler King** in his clearing.
7. Dawn over the Hollow: every lantern burns, and Hale gives you the
   Warden's Longbow.

## Weapons

Mostly ranged: the hunt is about the shot. At least these:

- Ranged: a crossbow (with a few bolt types), a lever-action hunting
  rifle, a recurve hunting bow, the Warden's Longbow.
- Melee: a woodsman's hatchet for when something gets too close.

## Enemies

**Levels 1 to 15**, levelled by hunting. Five bands of three levels,
tougher the deeper into the wilds you go, with elites along the way.
At least these:

- Common kinds: red deer (L1–3), wild boar (L4–6), elk (L7–9), lynx on
  the crags and brown bears in the den (L10–12), and the King's thralls
  (L13–15): moss-grown, glassy-eyed elk and boar he calls out of the
  fog, who roam the old-growth at night and flee the dawn.
- Rare coats among the commons: pale, black, scarred, huge.
- Elites, each with its own trick and a trophy: Old Ironhide, Terror
  of the Hollow, a charging boar (L6); the Imperial Bull, an elk whose
  bugle calls rivals at dusk (L9); Old Blackpaw, the bear who ambushes
  from the cave mouth (L12); the Ghost Stag, who fades and comes back
  behind you (L14).
- The final boss: **the Antler King** (L15), an ancient forest
  guardian gone wrong: bark-plated, a bone skull for a face, three
  lanterns in his antlers and a glowing amber ribcage. He comes only at
  night, and the fog closes around his clearing as the arena wall.
  Three phases, with thralls and falling lanterns, and an intro
  cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests, at least: the miller's errand (clear the thralls off
  the millrace at night so the wheel turns again), the trader's
  orders, and Hale's hunts.
- **The contract board** always shows a few hunts (a species, a rare
  coat, an elite, a thrall cull); finishing one draws the next.
- Harvest what you take: kneel and skin it, and ravens come for the
  rest. Elites and the King hang on the trophy wall with a joke title.
- **The hunter's journal**: a leather book of pencil sketches of every
  beast, elite and place you've found.
- No money: the trader barters hides, antlers and resin for special
  bolts, cartridges and finishes. Loot and a pack to carry it in.
- Amber resin on the pines, carved wooden tokens and secrets to find.
  Ravens and an owl fly toward places you haven't found yet.

## Look

Photoreal boreal forest. Tall Scots pines, white birch, giant firs and
cedars, granite crags, moss and needles underfoot, a still pond that
mirrors the sky. Real light: low gold sun through the trunks, dawn
ground fog, soft rain, blue moonlight, warm cabin windows and lanterns
glowing in the fog. Natural colour, eerie folklore at night, never
cartoon.
