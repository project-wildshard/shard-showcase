# Pine Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a deep boreal forest valley
so real you can smell the pine, and a folk tale after dark. Think Red
Dead Redemption 2's hunting, The Witcher 3's monsters and the
Night-Walker of Princess Mononoke. You are a hunter. By night come fog
and lanterns, and folk things walk the old trees. Quiet, beautiful and
unsettling, never gory, and epic all the way.

## Shape

It is one zone of a bigger MMO world, joined to its neighbours by
roads, so it fits inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube and never grows.
Four forest tracks enter at the middle of the cube's four sides,
halfway up, each running off the edge to the rest of the world;
nothing beyond needs to exist yet. Both mini dungeons sit inside the
cube too.

- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** The valley fills the
  full 500 × 500 m footprint and its full height, from the caves under
  the roots on the cube's floor to the granite crags and the crown of
  the elder pine at its top, trees above trees on every slope.
  Clearings are small and the forest closes in; open sky is never most
  of the view.
- **Every way across:** game trails, the river by canoe, ziplines
  between the crags, rope arrows up the tall trunks, runs along the
  canopy, and the root caves underneath.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Filled with biomes**, each with its own look, game and danger.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- A watermill hamlet, a hunting lodge, and a ranger's cabin whose back
  wall is the trophy wall.
- **The hunt**: read tracks and blood, mind the wind, call the beast
  in, kneel to skin it. A journal of pencil sketches of all you find.
- **The elder pine**: older and taller than the forest, roots in the
  caves, crown above the ridge. Climb it, then run the canopy.
- **Night is another forest**: fog, moving paths, folk things. Light
  is your weapon; every lantern you relight pushes the night back.
- Secrets and caches the ravens lead you to.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by hunting.
- Two mini dungeons: the root caves under the elder pine, hunted by
  lantern through its roots, and an old fire lookout on the high crag
  you fight up, landing by landing, with every lamp dark.

## Features

- Hunting and tracking.
- Trophies.
- Contracts and barter.
- Night and lanterns.
- Climbing the canopy.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps across the whole valley that ends in
an epic final boss fight; the final boss walks only at night. The
story, the steps and the boss are yours to design.

## Look

- Photoreal boreal forest: Scots pine, birch, giant firs, granite
  crags, a still pond. Natural colour by day, eerie folklore by night.
- Real light: low sun through the trunks, dawn fog, soft rain, blue
  moonlight, lanterns in the fog. Never storms, snow or cartoon.
- Red Dead Redemption 2's forests as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Ranged: a horn-and-birch hunting bow whose held full draw lets you
  feel the wind on the arrow; a trapper's crossbow whose bolts trail a
  bell, so you follow a wounded beast by ear through the fog; a lever
  rifle loaded with salt and silver for the night; and more. The bow
  is the heart: real draw, drop and wind.
- Melee: a woodsman's hatchet that fells a tree to bridge a gully; a
  boar spear whose crossbar stops a charge dead on its point; and
  more, for when it gets too close.
- Rope arrows climb the tall trunks; lantern arrows light the dark.

## Enemies

The enemies should include brown bears, boar that charge as a sounder,
lynx that drop from the crags, poachers who set snares, and the folk
things of the night fog, and more.

- Many enemies, each with a rare variant, a rare coat on every beast,
  and no wolves. Red deer and elk to hunt too. Elites, the valley's
  legends, are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings, by day and by night.
- A contract board at the lodge.
- No money: you barter hides, antlers and amber resin.
