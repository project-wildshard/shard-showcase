# Pine Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a deep, tall, dense boreal
forest so real you can smell the pine, and high above it, in the crowns
of the giant trees, a mystical elven realm, and deep below it, a dwarven
realm of mines and caves. Think Red Dead Redemption 2's forests, the old
forests of Princess Mononoke, the great tree cities of Guild Wars 2, and
the Great Deku Tree of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
Photorealistic from top to bottom. Quiet, beautiful and wild, never
gory, and epic from the first step to the last.

## Shape

It is one zone of a bigger MMO world, joined to its neighbours by
roads, so it fits inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube and never grows.
Four forest tracks enter at the middle of the cube's four sides,
halfway up, each running off the edge to the rest of the world;
nothing beyond needs to exist yet. All three mini dungeons sit
inside the cube too.

- **Three zones, stacked.** The forest floor is the human world:
  hunters, woodsmen and rangers. Above it, in and above the crowns of
  the tallest trees, is a mystical, fantastical elven realm, a city
  floating in the trees. Below it, a large cave network and the mines
  of the dwarves. Each is as big and worth exploring as the others.
- **The mana tree:** one colossal tree at the heart of it all, far
  bigger than any other, rising from the forest floor through the
  elven realm and above it, with a dungeon inside it.
- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** The forest fills the full
  500 × 500 m footprint and its full height, from the deepest dwarven
  mines on the cube's floor to the crown of the mana tree at its top.
  The trees are tall and dense and the forest closes in; open sky is
  never most of the view.
- **Every way across:** game trails, the rivers by canoe, mine carts and
  lifts, and the climbs between the three zones, with ziplines and rope
  bridges as extras.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- **On the ground, the human forest, a Red Dead Redemption 2 forest:**
  for example a watermill, a sawmill, a hunting lodge, rangers' cabins,
  ponds, beaver dams, rivers, hills and hunters' camps, and more.
- **In the trees and above them, the elven realm:** mystical halls,
  homes and markets built into the crowns of giant trees and strung
  between them, with their own people, quests and secrets. Reaching
  it the first time is a moment.
- **Under the ground, the dwarven realm:** a large cave network to
  explore, and the dwarves' mines, halls and forges, with their own
  people, quests and secrets.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over, on the ground and
  in the trees.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by fighting.
- Three mini dungeons, one for each realm: inside the mana tree, like
  the Great Deku Tree; a lost dwarven deep mine, far below the halls;
  and on the forest floor, an old fire lookout on the high crag,
  fought up landing by landing.

## Features

- Ranged only: bows, crossbows, rifles, pistols and revolvers, plus
  elven and dwarven weapons.
- A human forest, an elven realm in the trees above it and a dwarven
  realm in the mines below.
- A large cave network to explore.
- A colossal mana tree with a dungeon inside.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps across the whole forest, from the
mines to the elven realm, that ends in an epic final boss fight. The
story, the steps and the boss are yours to design.

## Look

- Photorealistic graphics, first and last: this zone should look
  like a real forest.
- Photoreal boreal forest: Scots pine, birch, giant firs, granite
  crags, a still pond, natural colour.
- Real light: low sun through the trunks, dawn fog, soft rain. Never
  storms, snow or cartoon.
- The elven realm above is mystical and fantastical, but built and lit
  as real as the forest below it.
- Red Dead Redemption 2's forests as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- **Ranged only; there is no melee.** A bow, a crossbow, a lever
  rifle, an AR-15 rifle, pistols and revolvers, elven and dwarven
  weapons, and more: every one heavy, loud and a joy to fire.

## Enemies

- Fill all three realms with enemies as you see fit: on the ground,
  in the trees and in the caves and mines.
- Like an MMO, they come in combat zones by level, each with its own
  part of the forest: 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 and 13–15.
- Then level 15 endgame content: bosses and more worth coming back
  for.
- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites, the forest's legends,
  are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings.
- Hunting quests and a hunting board from the human hunters.
