# Pine Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a deep boreal forest valley
so real you can smell the pine, and a folk tale after dark. Think Red
Dead Redemption 2's hunting, The Witcher 3's monsters and the
Night-Walker of Princess Mononoke. You are a hunter. By night come fog
and lanterns, and something with antlers of light walks the old trees.
Quiet, beautiful and unsettling, never gory, and epic all the way.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads are forest
  tracks that just lead off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by climbing the crags
  and the oldest trees and going down into the caves under the roots,
  never by making it bigger. The mini dungeon sits physically inside
  the cube and overlaps nothing else.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **Filled with biomes**, each with its own look, game and danger.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- A watermill hamlet, a hunting lodge, and a ranger's cabin whose back
  wall is the trophy wall.
- **The hunt**: read tracks and blood, mind the wind, call the beast
  in, kneel to skin it. A journal of pencil sketches of all you find.
- **The elder pine**: older and taller than the forest, roots in the
  caves, crown above the ridge. Climb it, then run the canopy.
- **Night is another forest**: fog, moving paths, folk things. Light
  is your weapon; every lantern you relight pushes the night back.
- A canoe, a zipline, and secrets and caches the ravens lead you to.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by hunting.
- A mini dungeon.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps, from the night the valley's lanterns
go dark to the dawn that relights them, ending in a final boss fight
worth the journey. The story, steps and boss are yours to design.

- **The final boss walks only at night.** From your first night its
  antler lights drift along the far ridge, too high for anything
  alive. When it comes, the fog swallows the forest and the fight is
  lit only by what it carries and what you carry. It ends at dawn.

## Look

- Photoreal boreal forest: Scots pine, birch, giant firs, granite
  crags, a still pond. Natural colour by day, eerie folklore by night.
- Real light: low sun through the trunks, dawn fog, soft rain, blue
  moonlight, lanterns in the fog. Never storms, snow or cartoon.
- Red Dead Redemption 2's forests as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Ranged: a hunting bow, a crossbow, a lever-action rifle, a great
  longbow and more. The bow is the heart: real draw, drop and wind.
- Melee: a woodsman's hatchet and more, for when it gets too close.
- Rope arrows climb the tall trunks; lantern arrows light the dark.

## Enemies

The enemies should include red deer, wild boar, elk, lynx on the crags
and brown bears, and more.

- Many beasts, each with a rare coat, and no wolves. Elites, the
  valley's legends, are scattered around. By night, the folk things.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings, by day and by night.
- A contract board at the lodge.
- No money: you barter hides, antlers and amber resin.
