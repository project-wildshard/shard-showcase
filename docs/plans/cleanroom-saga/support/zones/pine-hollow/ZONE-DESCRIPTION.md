# Pine Hollow

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a deep boreal forest valley
so real you can smell the pine, and a folk tale after dark. Think Red
Dead Redemption 2's hunting, The Witcher 3's monsters and the old
forests of Princess Mononoke. Photorealistic from top to bottom. You
are a hunter. By night come fog and lanterns, and folk things walk the
old trees. Quiet, beautiful and unsettling, never gory, and epic from
the first step to the last.

## Shape

It is one zone of a bigger MMO world, joined to its neighbours by
roads, so it fits inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube and never grows.
Four forest tracks enter at the middle of the cube's four sides,
halfway up, each running off the edge to the rest of the world;
nothing beyond needs to exist yet. All three mini dungeons sit inside
the cube too.

- **Use the whole cube, every corner of it.** The valley fills the
  full 500 × 500 m footprint and its full height, from the caves under
  the roots on the cube's floor to the granite crags and the crowns of
  the tallest trees at its top, trees above trees on every slope.
  Clearings are small and the forest closes in; open sky is never most
  of the view.
- **Every way across:** game trails, the rivers by canoe, climbs
  along the canopy and the root caves underneath, with ziplines and
  rope bridges as extras.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach.
- **A real forest, full of forest things:** ponds, beaver dams,
  rivers, hills, forest houses, mills, hunters' camps and more, each
  with its own look, game and danger, by day and by night.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- **The hunt is the heart.** Read tracks and blood, mind the wind,
  call the beast in, take the shot, kneel to skin it. Everything you
  find goes into a journal of pencil sketches, and the best of it goes
  on your trophy wall.
- **The elder pine:** older and taller than the forest, roots in the
  caves, crown above the ridge. Climb it, then run the canopy.
- **Light is your weapon at night.** Fog rolls in, the paths move and
  the folk things walk; every lantern you relight pushes the night
  back.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over, by day and by
  night.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by hunting.
- Three mini dungeons: the root caves under the elder pine, hunted by
  lantern; an old fire lookout on the high crag, fought up landing by
  landing with every lamp dark; and one that is only there at night.

## Features

- Hunting and tracking.
- Ranged only: crossbows, rifles and pistols.
- Trophies and a trophy wall.
- Contracts and barter, no money.
- Climbing the trees and running the canopy.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps across the whole valley, by day and
by night, that ends in an epic final boss fight; the final boss walks
only at night. The story, the steps and the boss are yours to design.

## Look

- Photorealistic graphics, first and last: this zone should look
  like a real forest.
- Photoreal boreal forest: Scots pine, birch, giant firs, granite
  crags, a still pond. Natural colour by day, eerie folklore by night.
- Real light: low sun through the trunks, dawn fog, soft rain, blue
  moonlight, lanterns in the fog. Never storms, snow or cartoon.
- Red Dead Redemption 2's forests as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- **Ranged only; there is no melee.** A crossbow, a scoped hunting
  rifle, a lever rifle loaded with silver for what walks at night, a
  pistol, and more: every one real, heavy and loud.

## Enemies

- Fill the valley with enemies as you see fit: by day and by night,
  on the ground, in the trees and under the roots. Great brown bears
  by day and the folk things of the night fog define it. No wolves.
- Like an MMO, they come in level bands, each with its own part of
  the valley: 1–3, 4–6, 7–9 and 10–12.
- Levels 13 to 15 are the endgame: bosses and endgame content worth
  coming back for.
- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Game to hunt too, not only
  foes. Elites, the valley's legends, are scattered around.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests.
- Happenings, by day and by night.
- A contract board at the lodge.
- No money: you barter hides, antlers and amber resin.
