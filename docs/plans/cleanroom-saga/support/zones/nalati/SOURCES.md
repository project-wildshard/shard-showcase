# Nalati Grasslands starter pack: sources

Pack: `~/projects/game-demos/nalati-clean-room`. Boss: the Golden King.
Levels 1–15.

- Read: SP `docs/design/nalati/geography-and-map.md` (bands, POIs,
  layout A), `elites-and-bosses.md` (five elites, Golden King and Jel
  Ata fights), `combat.md` (four weapons), `wolves-horses-taming.md`
  (taming, Tulpar), `stealth-and-storms.md` (headings), `look-pass.md`
  (painterly levers); `W/shard-nalati/src/data/questContent.json` (three
  chapters, NPC names); `docs/plans/GW2-ZONES.md` §2, §9.
- Main chain chosen: Tulpar folded into The Golden King (always open,
  not weather gated): meet the elder, break a horse, drive wolves off
  the flock, dusk balbals, the kurgan mini dungeon, the King, the plaque
  on the ribbon pole (a payoff GW2-ZONES says was promised and never
  built).
- Folded in as side content: Father of the Wind (storm feathers, Wind
  Cairn, Jel Ata) as the optional raid-style boss; kokpar, races and a
  courier ride; all five elites incl. Argymaq (tame, not kill).
- Added: levels 1–15 in five bands; foxes and wild boar as low-band
  commons; "kurgan dead"; side quests for each camp NPC; kokpar rank and
  medals unlock tack (GW2-ZONES proposal).
- Cut: Tas Ata (already dropped), sheep/flock-recovery event, horse
  panic and STEED bars, the eagle-hunter, wolf-raid schedule,
  horizon-ring tech, every number and coordinate.
