# Nalati Grasslands

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. A high Tian Shan steppe: a
braided river valley, a spruce escarpment, and above it the Sky
Grassland, vast and wind-combed, ending in snow mountains. Kazakh
herders in white felt yurts, horses, sheep and wolves. Its heart: tame
a wild horse and ride him, hunt from the saddle, hide in the tall
grass, ride out the storms, and wake the old kings.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by going under the
  kurgans and up the crags, never by making it bigger.
- The zone is a slice of a climb: the river valley in the north, the
  forested escarpment across the middle, the open Sky Grassland over
  the south half, snowy crags in one corner. Past the edge the
  grassland rolls on for miles to a wall of snow peaks.
- The great kurgan's halls physically sit under the mound, inside the
  cube, and overlap nothing. No portal leads outside the cube.
- The **valley is peaceful** by day. It gets tougher the further up
  and south you go.
- Day and night. Steppe storms with lightning roll in now and then,
  visible from anywhere as they build. At dusk the stone warriors wake;
  at night ghost riders ride the ridges.

## The steppe

- **The nomad camp** in the valley: yurts, a corral, a hitching
  rail, cooking smoke and a ribbon pole. Its people include **Baqyt
  Ata**, the elder; **Dauren** and **Erlan**, the herders; **Gulnar
  Apa**, the cook, who also trades; and **Ayan**, a boy who wants to
  ride. Beside it, **the Kunes**: braided channels, a timber bridge,
  a ford and a sheep pasture.
- **The spruce gullies**, with a wolf den, and a road that switchbacks
  up to the rim. **Eagle Rock**, a granite tor with the best view.
- **The Sky Grassland**: open and treeless, wind waves in the grass,
  flowers and cloud shadows. Crouch in its tall grass and wolves lose
  you (eagles see from above). The wild herd roams the horse plains;
  the Wind Cairn stands on the south rim.
- **The kurgan field**: grassy burial mounds, a ring of old stone
  warriors (balbals) on a knoll, and the great kurgan.
- **The crags**: snowy granite ledges and a leopard's cave.
- Peaceful things: ride (your horse comes when you whistle); kokpar
  against the herders; horse races and a courier ride with medal
  times; help with the flock; tame more horses; vistas and secrets.

## Main quest: The Golden King

At least these steps, each marked on the map and in the world:

1. Cross the bridge and meet Baqyt Ata at the camp.
2. **Tulpar**: break a wild stallion on the horse plains. Come in slow,
   win his trust, then hold on while he bucks. He is yours to name.
3. Ride out with Dauren to drive a wolf pack off the flock, and learn
   to shoot from the saddle.
4. Topple the balbal warriors that wake at dusk; each one gives up a
   piece of the kurgan's old story.
5. Find the way into the great kurgan, the mini dungeon: a looter's
   tunnel, a timber corridor of sleeping stone guards, gold everywhere.
6. Beat **the Golden King** and take the Golden Bow from his coffin.
7. Bring a gold plaque home for the ribbon pole, and the camp feasts.

Then an optional raid-style boss: win storm feathers from the elites,
tie them to the Wind Cairn in a storm, and fight **Jel Ata, Father of
the Wind**, a giant of storm cloud, on horseback.

## Weapons

At least these, on foot and from the saddle:

- Melee: the sabre, the spear (a lance at the gallop).
- Ranged: the recurve bow, throwing javelins, the Golden Bow.

## Enemies

**Levels 1 to 15**, levelled by fighting. Five bands of three levels,
tougher the higher and further south you go, with elites along the
way. At least these:

- Common kinds: young wolves and steppe foxes in the valley (L1–3);
  wild boar in the spruce (L4–6); wolf packs that hunt you through the
  grass (L7–9); balbal warriors with amber cracks the sabre and spear
  break (L10–12); the kurgan's restless dead, and ghost riders on the
  ridges at night (L13–15).
- Rare variants among the commons, and night variants of each.
- Elites, each a different test, with a trophy and a joke title:
  Kokbori, Mother of the Pack, a sky-grey she-wolf (L5); Argymaq the
  Unbroken, a black stallion you beat, then tame as the best horse
  there is (L8); Aqbars the Pale, a snow leopard on the ledges (L11);
  Qyran the Storm-Wing, an eagle who hunts only in storms (L13); Qara
  Batyr the Unburied, captain of the night riders (L14).
- The final boss: **the Golden King** (L15), a Saka king in gold
  armour rising from his coffin in a shaft of daylight. Three phases,
  with waking stone guards and pouring sand, and an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests from the camp, at least: Gulnar Apa's cooking, Ayan's
  first ride, Erlan's lost sheep, and the herders' kokpar ladder.
- Loot, hides and trophies. Gulnar Apa trades. Kokpar rank and race
  medals unlock saddles and tack. Achievements with joke titles.

## Look

Painterly steppe, like a living gouache painting: a warm low sun and a
cool sky, soft painted light, shadows that go blue and violet, never
grey. Sky-blue haze layering the distant hills, sunlit clouds with
their shadows sliding over the grass, wind waves, wildflower drifts,
white felt yurts, the snow range pale on the horizon. The art
direction is shown in the three images in `concept/`.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
