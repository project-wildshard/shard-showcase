# Nalati Grasslands

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone: a high Tian Shan valley, a
Kazakh herders' steppe climbing from a braided river to a grassland in
the sky under snow peaks. Think Ghost of Tsushima, Shadow of the
Colossus, and the films Mongol and Nomad: The Warrior. Grass in wind
waves to the horizon, white felt yurts, a wild herd thundering past,
storms you watch coming from anywhere. A land lived on horseback.

**It must look and feel strongly inspired by the real places:** the
Nalati Grasslands, the Sky Grasslands, Snow Lotus Valley and Panlong
Valley. Study them, and make anyone who has been there recognise them.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads just lead
  off the edge; nothing beyond needs to exist.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level at its middle height. Fit more in by climbing to the snow
  crags and going down under the burial mounds, never by making it
  bigger. The mini dungeon sits physically inside the cube and
  overlaps nothing else.
- **One open zone**, no fixed sides: go anywhere, see anything you can
  reach, and ride almost all of it.
- **Filled with biomes**, each with its own look, life and danger.
- **Five or more epic set pieces**: moments a player remembers and
  wants to show someone.
- Each set piece must work as a ten-second clip, filmed on a phone in
  portrait, that makes someone ask "what game is that?"

## In the zone

To get a feeling for what to build, here are some ideas for the zone
that you can start with:

- A nomad camp of white felt yurts, a corral and a ribbon pole.
- **Horses are the heart**: break a wild stallion and name him; whistle
  and he comes at a gallop. Kokpar, races and a courier ride.
- **The wild herd**: hundreds of horses running free. Ride into the
  stampede, pick one, tame it.
- **A golden eagle** on your arm: cast it up to see the steppe from the
  sky, mark prey and danger, and strike.
- **The storms are alive**: they build in plain sight and roll in with
  lightning. A giant walks inside some, fought only from the saddle.
- Tall grass hides you from wolves. At dusk the stone warriors wake;
  at night ghost riders ride the ridges.
- Secrets, vistas, caches and collectibles all over.
- Levels 1 to 15, levelled by fighting.
- A mini dungeon.

## Main quest

An epic quest line in many steps, from the herders' camp across the
whole steppe, on foot and on horseback, ending in a final boss fight
worth the journey. The story, steps and boss are yours to design.

- **The final boss sleeps in gold under the greatest burial mound.**
  The fight bursts out of the tomb onto the steppe and ends at a gallop.

## Look

- Painterly, a living gouache: a warm low sun, a cool sky, soft
  painted light, shadows that go blue and violet, never grey.
- Sky-blue haze layering the far hills, cloud shadows sliding over the
  grass, wildflower drifts, the snow range pale on the horizon.
- Ghost of Tsushima's fields as the inspiration.

## Weapons

- Melee: a sabre, a spear that becomes a lance at the gallop, and more.
- Ranged: a recurve horse bow, throwing javelins and more.
- Every weapon works from the saddle: lean out to cut at a gallop,
  turn and shoot behind you like the old horse archers.

## Enemies

- Many enemies, each with a rare variant. Elites are scattered around.
- Wolf packs, leopards on the crags, stone warriors, restless dead.
- A final boss at the end of the main quest, at level 15.

## Quests

- Side quests from the camp.
- Happenings.
- A bounty board, plus kokpar ranks and race medals that win new tack.
