# Driftwood Isle

A single hand-made MMO zone, played alone. A small tropical island in
open turquoise sea, under a huge ringed planet. You arrive by sailboat
at a wooden pier with nothing but a driftwood sword. A castaway tells
you the old stone ring in the jungle is sealed, and three glyph stones
hidden around the island will open it. Open it, and the Drowned
Captain rises. It should feel sunny, cute and adventurous, a little
spooky at night, with a sea you can swim forever and never drown in.

## Shape

- It is a real zone of a bigger MMO world: a grid of 500 × 500 m zones
  joined by roads. So it is exactly 500 × 500 m, with four road
  entrances, one at the middle of each edge. Here the roads are
  jetties and sandbars that just lead off the edge; nothing beyond
  needs to exist. Every jetty has something worth walking out for.
- **Everything lives inside one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube**, with the
  road level (the sea surface) at its middle height. Fit more in by
  going under the sea and into the cliffs, never by making it bigger.
- The wreck's hold and the sea cave are interiors, but each physically
  sits inside the cube, and no two spaces overlap. No portal leads
  outside the cube.
- The **south shore is peaceful**: the pier, the beach and the camp.
  Danger grows inland, toughest at the east cove and the jungle.
- A long bright day, golden hour, and a short moonlit night that is
  blue and playable, never black. Warm tropical squalls blow through.
  Some enemies only come out at night.

## The island

- **The pier**: your sailboat moored at its end, and the crescent
  beach it lands on.
- **Wendell's camp**: the castaway's stilt hut on green terraces above
  the beach, with a campfire whose smoke you can see from anywhere.
  **Maren** keeps a trading stall on the sand below. Old **Tomas**
  fishes off the north jetty.
- **The lookout**: a watchtower with a beacon on the tallest basalt
  cliff, over a swaying rope bridge. A long zipline runs down to the
  cove.
- **Wreck Cove**: a half-sunk ship on the reef, tidepools, a waterfall,
  and a sea cave with a glow inside.
- **The Ring Shrine**: a jungle clearing with a stone monolith whose
  round opening frames the planet, glowing glyphs, fireflies and a
  spring pool. Sealed.
- Peaceful things: swim anywhere and dive the reef for pearls and
  sunken chests; sail the boat around the island; beat your own best
  time on the island trials (the lookout climb, the wreck run, the cave
  run) for bronze, silver and gold; sea glass, bottles with notes,
  buried chests, vistas and secrets. Gulls fly toward places you have
  not found yet.

## Main quest: The Sealed Ring

At least these steps, each marked on the map and in the world:

1. Step off the boat, fight a practice crab, and meet Wendell.
2. **The lookout glyph**: cross the rope bridge, climb the tower and
   light the beacon.
3. **The wreck glyph**: ride the zipline to the cove and go down into
   the wreck's hold, the mini dungeon: keys, levers, a pump and a winch
   below deck, the drowned crew, and the Drowned Bosun guarding the
   glyph and the iron sword.
4. **The cave glyph**: a tide puzzle in the sea cave.
5. Bring the three glyph stones to the Ring Shrine. It opens, and the
   Drowned Captain rises from the spring pool.
6. Beat **the Drowned Captain**, then watch golden hour fill the stone
   ring with the planet.

## Weapons

At least these:

- Melee: the driftwood sword, the iron sword, a boat-hook spear.
- Ranged: a sling, a flintlock pistol, a harpoon gun that also works
  underwater.

## Enemies

**Levels 1 to 10**, levelled by fighting. Five bands of two levels,
tougher the further from the pier you go, with an elite at each band
gate. At least these:

- Common kinds: reef crabs with hard shells in front (L1–2); boars
  that charge (L3–4); coconut monkeys in troops that throw coconuts
  from the palms (L5–6); bears (L7–8); drowned sailors (L9–10),
  glowing cyan, in the wreck and on the beaches at night.
- Rare variants among the commons, and night variants of each.
- Elites: King Pinch, a giant reef crab (L2); Old Tusk, the boar that
  won't stay down (L4); Big Mango, boss of the monkey troop (L6);
  Grumbles, the brown bear of the jungle (L8); the Drowned Bosun (L9).
- A night elite: Lantern Jack, a drowned lookout who walks the beach
  with a green lantern you can see from the camp.
- The final boss: **the Drowned Captain** (L10), barnacled and huge,
  in the shrine pool. Three phases and an intro cutscene.

## Quests and economy

- Side quests from Wendell, Maren and Tomas, at least: the monkey troop
  raiding Maren's stall (again and again, signalled from the camp),
  pearl dives for Tomas, the bottle trail, and the bear's trophy.
- A bounty notice at the camp pays for each enemy kind, elite and the
  boss, with a joke title the first time.
- The currency is doubloons. Maren sells weapons unlocked by level,
  charms and cosmetics. Loot, chests and keys, and a pack.

## Look

Faceted, flat-shaded low-poly, bright and tropical. Toon lighting with
coloured shadows and a rim of light on every edge, like a storybook
cartoon adventure: turquoise shallows fading to deep blue, white foam,
lush greens, warm sand, a painted gradient sky with chunky faceted
clouds, and the ringed planet always hanging over the sea. Never
photoreal, never grey. The art direction is shown in the three images
in `concept/`.

## HUD

Mobile friendly, built for a phone held in portrait, with all the
normal MMO features.
